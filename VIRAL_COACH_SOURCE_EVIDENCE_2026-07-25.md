## Terms and guarantee evidence gap

The public [Website Terms of Use](https://www.viralcoach.com/site-terms), dated April 29, 2026, state that an enrolled client’s relationship is separately governed by the Service Agreement Terms & Conditions, individual Program Agreement or Membership Agreement, and applicable product addenda; the product-specific addendum controls a conflict. The website terms themselves do not define the million-view guarantee. They also reserve Viral Coach intellectual-property rights, describe only a limited revocable client license, permit use of enrolled-client names, likenesses, testimonials, and public success metrics subject to the controlling agreement, and cap website-use liability at $100 while deferring enrolled-client liability terms to the service agreement.

Therefore, the full written proposal and enrolled-client agreement—not the public “Guaranteed 1M Views” headline—must control Beacon’s diligence. Search results and third-party snippets are not substitutes for that agreement. Before Beacon considers an engagement, Viral Coach should provide a current written proposal/master service agreement or order form that defines: the view metric; platforms; qualifying accounts; timeline; content/posts/ad spend; client duties; analytics access; the remedy if the threshold is missed; exclusions; cancellation/refund rules; ownership; platform-policy compliance; account access; data processing; indemnity; limitation of liability; testimonial and likeness rights; and post-termination asset/credential handling.

## Data, AI, and account-access evidence

The public [Privacy Policy](https://www.viralcoach.com/privacy), dated April 29, 2025, says that Viral Coach may process contact and account/usage data, recordings of strategy and onboarding interactions with prior notice, prompts/content/outputs for AI-assisted features, and marketing/analytics data. It says AI inputs and outputs may be processed by Viral Coach and third-party AI providers under contractual confidentiality and data-processing limitations; it also says it does not use client inputs to train publicly available AI models and does not use solely automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects.

These are useful public commitments, not a Beacon-specific data-processing agreement. Before a pilot, Beacon must receive the named processor/subprocessor list, the applicable agreement/addendum, role allocation, retention/deletion schedule, security controls, access-log and incident-notification commitments, recording consent flow, geographic processing locations, and an account-access protocol that preserves Beacon’s direct administrative ownership. No member, client, prospect, payment, or sensitive operational data should be supplied during early vendor evaluation.

## Preliminary evidence conclusion

The service may be a legitimate content-coaching/production vendor with substantial social proof, but the available evidence supports only a **hold-for-contractual-due-diligence** posture. The headline “Guaranteed 1M Views” is an engagement promise that must not be interpreted as a lead, revenue, conversion, audience-quality, brand-safety, or policy-compliance guarantee. Any future pilot must make organic, policy-compliant, high-quality attention—not raw views—the non-negotiable objective.
