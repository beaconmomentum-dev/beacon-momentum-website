# Social-Ready Beacon Editorial Inventory

## Current Count

**12 public destinations are ready for social distribution today:** the ten narrated August Signal articles, the standalone **How Beacon Works** field brief, and the Foundation Year page. The **Five Questions That Keep You in Charge** field note is already represented by its August Signal article; it should be promoted as one idea with one primary article URL rather than treated as a duplicate social destination.

## Distribution Rule

Every public social post should lead to an article or field brief first. The destination page may then offer one contextual Beacon next step. Public editorial should not lead directly to a generic sales offer, a support-payment page, or an unrelated B2B consultation route.

## Social-Readiness Boundary

Each August article has a compliant 16:9 editorial image, an article-owned MP3 narration, a transcript, a WebVTT caption track, a canonical route, and source-bounded public copy. These elements make the batch ready for postable social traffic.

The article component now updates its runtime title, description, canonical URL, and Open Graph/Twitter metadata. The existing static SPA delivery still provides default raw-HTML metadata to crawlers that do not execute JavaScript. A future prerender or SSR implementation would be required for crawler-specific per-article Open Graph cards on every platform.

## Explicit Exclusions

Do not use internal pilot pages, private workflow records, unpublished update reviews, Foundation Year support checkout, or Beacon Labs service routes as default social targets. Use Beacon Labs only where the public article itself names a clear organizational implementation need.
