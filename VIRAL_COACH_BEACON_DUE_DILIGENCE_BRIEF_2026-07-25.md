# Viral Coach: Beacon Social-Growth Vendor Due-Diligence Brief

**Prepared for:** Bob / Beacon Momentum, LLC
**Decision status:** **Do not engage on the public headline alone. Permit a no-commitment diligence conversation only if Viral Coach will provide its complete written offer and governing agreement in advance.**
**Purpose:** Determine whether Viral Coach’s “Guaranteed 1M Views” offer merits consideration as a Beacon social-media-growth vendor.

## Executive recommendation

Viral Coach appears to be a real coaching, content-production, and growth-service vendor with a sizable public marketing presence, client-facing programs, some independent review signals, and a public privacy-policy framework. That is enough to justify a **short diligence conversation** if Beacon wants to understand its operating model. It is **not** enough to justify a purchase, public endorsement, access to Beacon accounts, or reliance on the “Guaranteed 1M Views” headline.[1]

The correct stance is **conditional exploration, not a commitment**. Beacon should request the actual proposal, service agreement, product addendum, scope, metrics definition, delivery plan, and remedy before discussing price or granting any access. If Viral Coach cannot define a qualifying view, campaign duration, required volume, paid-media component, eligibility, exclusions, ownership, remedy, cancellation right, data handling, and platform-compliance method in writing, Beacon should decline.

> **The core question is not whether Viral Coach can generate one million views. It is whether it can generate policy-compliant, on-brand, qualified attention that Beacon owns and can convert into durable audience relationships.**

## What the public evidence supports

| Area | Evidence-supported finding | What the evidence does **not** establish |
|---|---|---|
| **Vendor identity and scope** | Viral Coach publicly markets social positioning, creative strategy, editing/production, performance marketing, organic-traffic systems, multi-platform distribution, coaching, and community/program components.[1] | The exact delivery model for Beacon, account staffing, content volume, platform mix, production responsibility, or business-outcome accountability. |
| **Million-view headline** | The vendor publicly uses “Guaranteed 1M Views” and reports averages/case-study outcomes.[1] | The definition of a view, qualifying accounts, time period, paid versus organic attribution, client obligations, exclusions, calculation method, or contractual remedy. |
| **Public disclaimers** | The home page states results are not guaranteed or typical and makes no guarantee of financial results.[1] | Whether a separate client agreement creates a valid, enforceable view-threshold remedy; the public terms expressly defer enrolled-client terms to separate agreements. |
| **Independent signals** | Trustpilot includes favorable experience narratives, and a verified Clutch review describes coaching and technical support on a small project.[1] | That Beacon will receive a comparable service, that the guarantee is performed, or that a comparable campaign will yield qualified audience, leads, or revenue. |
| **Data and AI posture** | The public privacy policy says it may process recordings and AI inputs/outputs, uses third-party AI providers under described limitations, says it does not train publicly available AI models on client inputs, and says humans remain responsible for significant decisions.[1] | Beacon-specific processing roles, subprocessor list, security controls, retention/deletion commitments, incident notice, access logs, or rights in a signed data-processing agreement. |

## Why Beacon should be cautious

| Risk | Why it matters for Beacon | Required answer or control |
|---|---|---|
| **Headline ambiguity** | A raw-view target is not equivalent to qualified reach, newsletter growth, enrollment interest, conversion, trust, or brand safety. | Make success criteria outcome-oriented and campaign-specific; the million-view figure cannot be Beacon’s primary success metric. |
| **Public disclaimer versus headline** | The plain-language promise and “results are not guaranteed” disclosure create a material diligence gap. | Obtain the agreement/addendum that defines the actual guarantee and remedy. The signed documents—not the website—must govern. |
| **Platform-policy exposure** | YouTube prohibits artificial metric inflation and says a hired promoter’s conduct can affect the client’s channel; Instagram prohibits inauthentic engagement tools; TikTok restricts certain harmful/unverified content.[2] [3] [4] | Written no-bots/no-fake-engagement/no-engagement-pods/no-deceptive-distribution warranty, source-analytics access, termination right, and indemnity/incident process. |
| **Brand and editorial control** | Viral hooks can create reach while diluting Beacon’s long-term credibility or causing claim-compliance issues. | Beacon retains final approval over scripts, captions, thumbnails, paid creative, trends, calls to action, and platform posts. |
| **Account, asset, and data control** | Delegated publishing, analytics, recordings, AI tools, and marketing systems can create credential, privacy, IP, and continuity risk. | Beacon keeps direct admin ownership; use least-privilege access; define asset/editable-file ownership, data retention, deletion, and post-termination handoff. |
| **Unit economics** | The public sources do not establish Beacon’s likely cost per qualified subscriber, qualified organization, or member request. | Run a time-bounded pilot only with a predefined incremental-baseline measurement plan and no unsupported ROI claim. |

## Beacon-specific decision

### Recommended now: a contract-first diligence call

It is reasonable for Beacon to take one exploratory sales call **only after** Viral Coach sends a current written proposal and its governing service/program agreement or at least a representative redacted version. Beacon should make clear that it is assessing a controlled social-growth pilot, not buying “one million views.” No staff, credentials, source videos, customer lists, member data, CRM exports, payment data, private analytics, or access to posting accounts should be provided during this evaluation stage.

### Not recommended now: buy, announce, or delegate channel control

Beacon should not sign an agreement, prepay a large program, grant account credentials, publish a vendor endorsement, or make a public claim based on the million-view headline until the written terms and pilot controls below are satisfied. A view guarantee without a qualified-attention definition risks buying the least useful type of traffic: attention that cannot be verified, owned, or converted.

## Conditions for a limited pilot

If written diligence is satisfactory, Beacon could consider a **limited, cancelable pilot** rather than a broad engagement. The pilot should begin with one clearly defined channel or content series, a measured pre-pilot baseline, and no claim that results are representative or guaranteed.

| Pilot control | Minimum standard |
|---|---|
| **Success metric** | Track qualified outcomes such as completed views by the intended audience, saves/shares, profile visits, email opt-ins, qualified organization inquiries, and attributable enrollment interest—not raw views alone. |
| **Baseline and reporting** | Capture at least 30 days of pre-pilot data where feasible. Require platform-native analytics and a recurring report that separates organic, paid, boosted, partner, and reposted distribution. |
| **Content approval** | Beacon has final written approval over every script, edit, caption, thumbnail, call to action, and paid placement. No unverified claims, manipulated testimonials, sensational fear framing, or policy-sensitive content. |
| **Distribution integrity** | No bots, view farms, purchased engagement, engagement pods, undisclosed incentives, credential sharing, or deceptive traffic sources. Require a platform-by-platform method statement. |
| **Account and asset ownership** | Beacon retains direct owner/admin control; vendors receive least-privilege access. Beacon owns finished and editable deliverables, source files, captions, analytics exports, and audience data produced for the engagement. |
| **Data and security** | Sign an appropriate data-processing and confidentiality arrangement. Prohibit uploading member/client/prospect/payment data to vendor tools without approved data flows and named subprocessors. |
| **Commercial protection** | Use a limited term, defined deliverables, clear cancellation and cure rights, a specific missed-guarantee remedy, and no automatic renewal or testimonial/likeness right beyond explicit written consent. |

## Five questions Beacon should send before spending money

1. **What exactly qualifies as a “view” for the one-million-view guarantee, on which platforms and accounts, during what measurement period, and how will Beacon inspect the platform-native proof?**
2. **What client duties, content volume, posting cadence, ad spend, approvals, niche exclusions, account-history conditions, and other prerequisites apply—and what happens if Beacon completes them and the threshold is not reached?**
3. **Please provide the proposed service agreement, program agreement, product addendum, cancellation/refund clause, guarantee remedy, limitation of liability, testimonial/likeness rights, asset ownership terms, and post-termination handoff terms.**
4. **What specific controls prohibit artificial engagement or policy-risky distribution, and will Viral Coach warrant compliance with YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and other applicable platform rules while providing source analytics by traffic source?**
5. **Which people, tools, AI providers, subcontractors, and platforms will process Beacon content or data; what access, retention, deletion, security, geographic-processing, incident-notification, and data-processing commitments will apply?**

## Final verdict

**Worth investigating? Yes, but only at the contract-first diligence stage. Worth doing now? No.** The vendor may be capable and legitimate, but the publicly available materials do not establish the conditions that matter most to Beacon: qualified attention, brand control, platform integrity, account ownership, conversion economics, and enforceable remedy. A carefully constrained pilot could be worthwhile only after those terms are written and accepted.
