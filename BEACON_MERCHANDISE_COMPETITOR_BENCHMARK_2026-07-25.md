# Beacon Merchandise Competitor Benchmark

**Prepared:** July 25, 2026  
**Purpose:** Identify observable merchandise categories beyond shirts among relevant education, creator-community, personal-development, and purpose-led brands. This is a catalog benchmark, not a demand forecast or legal/tax assessment.

## Verified current catalog patterns

| Brand | Observable categories beyond shirts | What the pattern demonstrates | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Daily Stoic** | Medallions, pendants, signed books, premium editions, challenge deck, art, and courses | A mature purpose-led education brand can extend beyond apparel through **ritual objects**, collectible symbols, and intellectual tools tied tightly to its core philosophy. | [Daily Stoic Store](https://store.dailystoic.com/) |
| **Foundr** | Mug, posters, sweatshirts, snapback hat | A creator/entrepreneur brand uses a compact physical line built around **desk utility**, visible affiliation, and motivational wall art. | [Foundr Merch](https://merch.foundr.com/) |

## Implications for Beacon

The strongest recurring non-shirt categories are not a generic “everything store.” They fall into three role-based families:

| Family | Observed examples | Beacon-specific translation | Launch suitability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Daily-use / desk utility** | Mug, water bottle, calendar, notebook-like workbooks | Signal Journal or Field Notebook; Coastline Tumbler or Water Bottle | Strong, provided fulfillment is simple |
| **Visible affiliation** | Cap, hoodie, sweatshirt, socks | Beacon cap or crewneck after the flagship shirt validates demand | Suitable after initial validation |
| **Meaning / ritual object** | Medallion, pendant, bracelet, challenge deck | Beacon Roundel medallion or wallet-sized field card | High brand fit; defer until the visual system and unit economics are proven |
| **Practical learning aid** | Book, workbook, guide, courses | Printed Navigator or digital field guide | Strong if it directly supports the education promise |

## Initial interpretation

The evidence supports a deliberately small Beacon line rather than an immediate broad assortment. A premium Supporter Edition T-shirt is a defensible flagship. The most coherent later physical additions are a **Signal Journal/Field Notebook**, a **Coastline Tumbler or Water Bottle**, a **cap or crewneck**, and eventually a **Beacon Roundel medallion or field card**. Pens and generic novelty items are common in corporate merchandise but were not a consistent differentiator across the verified catalogs.
