# Beacon Ecosystem Architecture and B2B Top-of-Funnel

**Authority:** Owner instruction, July 25, 2026
**Status:** Canonical operating model for public routing, membership ownership, and B2B discovery

## System of record

| Entity or property | Canonical role | What belongs there | What does not belong there |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Beacon Momentum, LLC** | Parent company | Brand governance, portfolio standards, shared disclosures, and cross-property operating policy | A single undifferentiated audience, shared customer records by default, or forced account unification |
| **`beaconmomentum.com`** | Forward-facing public ecosystem landing page | Brand story, audience-path selection, public education, proof, and an explicit B2B discovery entry | Membership accounts, member forums, course delivery, community data, or B2B checkout/intake forms |
| **`beaconcommunity.net`** | Actual membership platform and system of record for all membership-facing functionality | Enrollment, member accounts, community, courses, peer interaction, member AI/chat tools, check-ins, and all membership operations | B2B client sales, Labs payment flow, B2B strategy intake, or organization-client delivery |
| **`beaconlabs.ai`** | Distinct B2B operating arm | Organization diagnostics, pricing, sales, contracts, payment, intake, client data, implementation, and support | B2C membership enrollment, member records, or an implied membership benefit |
| **`app.beaconmomentum.com`** | Related technical surface requiring owner-authorized role inventory | Only the technical role the owner confirms after an administrative review | Public designation as the membership system of record or a default public handoff |

> **Operating rule:** `beaconmomentum.com` introduces the ecosystem. `beaconcommunity.net` operates the membership. `beaconlabs.ai` sells and delivers B2B work. The properties share ownership and brand standards, but they do not share customer or member data by default.

## Approved public paths

```text
Public visitor arrives at beaconmomentum.com
        │
        ├── “Membership & community” → beaconcommunity.net
        │       └── membership enrollment, account, courses, forum, chat, and member operations
        │
        └── “For organizations” → Beacon Labs services and investment snapshot
                └── explicit external handoff → beaconlabs.ai
                        └── B2B discovery, diagnostic, payment, intake, contract, and delivery
```

The B2B entry should remain visible enough to serve an organization that reaches the public landing page, but it must not displace the B2C path or obscure the fact that Beacon Labs is a separate business-services engagement. A qualified Community member may also use the same explicit external B2B handoff, but membership status must not be transferred, assumed, or used to prefill a Labs intake.

## B2B top-of-funnel on `beaconmomentum.com`

The public landing page should include a clearly labeled **“For Organizations”** destination in its primary navigation or an equally prominent route. Its page or expandable section should be titled **“Beacon Labs: AI Infrastructure for Organizations”** and use the disclosure: **“B2B services offered by Beacon Labs, a Beacon Momentum, LLC company.”**

It should provide a concise service-and-investment snapshot, organized by business outcome rather than a consumer-membership curriculum. Each card may link to a corresponding scoped Beacon Labs page, while the commercial action remains on `beaconlabs.ai`.

| B2B service group | Publicly listed Beacon Labs offering | Starting investment to represent | Public-site route and CTA |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Diagnose** | Beacon Signal Check | **$497 one-time** currently listed on the Beacon Labs homepage; do not syndicate until the conflicting free diagnostic routes are resolved | “Compare diagnostic options” → `beaconlabs.ai` |
| **Build automation** | Autonomous Business Stack | **From $5,500** one-time setup | “Explore automation systems” → `beaconlabs.ai` |
| **Rehearse a launch** | Scenario Rehearsal | **$1,200 per scenario** | “Rehearse a scenario” → `beaconlabs.ai` |
| **Develop an agent** | Custom Agent Development | **From $10,000 per project** | “Discuss an agent build” → `beaconlabs.ai` |
| **Install operating cadence** | AI COO System | **$1,997 setup + $97/month** | “Explore AI COO” → `beaconlabs.ai` |
| **Build the digital operation** | Web and app engineering; automation; AI video | **From $5,000 per project; $1,500/month automation; $800/video** | “Plan an implementation” → `beaconlabs.ai` |
| **Enterprise or specialized work** | NemoClaw Enterprise Deployment and VSO Technology Partner Program | **Custom / partnership-based** | “Request an enterprise consultation” → `beaconlabs.ai` |

The pricing table is a **transparent starting-investment snapshot**, not an order form. It must say that scope, availability, and final terms are confirmed by Beacon Labs. The B2C site may use a non-identifying referral parameter such as `source=beaconmomentum`; it must not embed Labs payments, use B2B intake forms, transfer B2C/member records, prefill form fields, or silently merge audience tags.

## Required content and routing standards

| Topic | Required standard |
|---|---|
| **B2C membership CTA** | “Membership & community” must link to `beaconcommunity.net`, not a generic public-site route or `app.beaconmomentum.com`. |
| **B2B CTA** | “For organizations” must open a clearly distinct Beacon Labs journey with the B2B disclosure visible before a form or purchase action. |
| **Pricing integrity** | The public Beacon Momentum snapshot must mirror one approved Labs price list. The current Signal Check conflict—free on dedicated diagnostic routes and $497 on the homepage—must be resolved before the price is syndicated. |
| **Data separation** | No shared accounts, automatic enrollment, default CRM merge, prefilled intake, or member-to-client data transfer. A person consents separately at the destination property. |
| **Brand relationship** | All properties may state: “A Beacon Momentum, LLC company” or equivalent approved parent-company language. They should retain division-specific labels that make B2C membership and B2B client services unambiguous. |
| **App-subdomain status** | No new public handoff to `app.beaconmomentum.com` until the owner-authorized technical inventory confirms its intended role, content ownership, data boundary, and relationship to Beacon Community. |

## Implementation order

| Phase | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **0 — governance** | Approve the single Labs offer/price ladder, publish suitable privacy and terms pages, confirm post-payment gating, and document B2C/member/B2B data boundaries. |

| **2 — controlled commercial handoff** | Route each B2B card to the relevant Labs page with explicit destination disclosure and non-identifying attribution only. |
| **3 — proof and optimization** | Add a validated case study, measure click-through and qualified inquiry quality, and improve the routing without merging profiles. |
