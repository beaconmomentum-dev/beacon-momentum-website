The Quiet Advantage of a System You Can Explain.

There is a quiet kind of advantage in being able to explain your own system. You know what it is for, where the work lives, who reviews it, and what happens when something changes. That clarity is not anti-technology. It is what lets technology become useful without becoming mysterious. Explain the work in ordinary language. What does this workflow do? What does it not do? Where does the record go? Who is responsible for the final decision? If those questions cannot be answered simply, the system is carrying too much hidden complexity. Accumulate judgment, not just tools. Tools change quickly. The ability to compare evidence, recognize a limitation, and choose a responsible next step compounds across every tool change. Keep the light on. A durable system does not promise certainty. It gives people a steadier next move because the work is visible, reviewable, and grounded in lived judgment.
