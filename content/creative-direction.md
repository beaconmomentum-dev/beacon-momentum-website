## Production Constants (All Videos)
- Deep navy (#0A1628) solid backgrounds
- White (#FFFFFF) primary text
- Gold (#B8860B) accent color
- Serif headlines (Playfair Display or similar)
- Sans-serif body (Inter or similar)
- Hard cuts only — zero transitions
- No background music
- Slow opacity fade-in for text (0.5s)
- 1920x1080 output, 30fps
- Editorial navigation bar on each slide
- 5-second black hold before end card
