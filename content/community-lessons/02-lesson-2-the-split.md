# LESSON 2: THE SPLIT

**Machine on the build side, you on the trust side.**

---

Every platform we sell on has quietly split your business into two halves. And they are pushing you in opposite directions depending on which half you are standing in.

On the side where you are buying, they can't give you machines fast enough. Google took keywords out of AI Max and let Gemini read your landing page instead. Meta will accept a URL and a dollar amount and build the entire campaign. TikTok will take a brief and hand you back a finished campaign. OpenAI will match your ad to a conversation based on a paragraph you typed. All of that is free, or nearly free, and it gets better every quarter without you doing anything.

On the side where you are being trusted, they are putting up fences. TikTok Shop banned synthetic voices on selling lives and capped avatars at half the frame. Meta wants AI content labeled and restricts monetization on repetitive machine output. Amazon put disclosure requirements on Kindle books. YouTube leadership named managing AI slop a priority for the year. And since August 2026, European law requires you to say when a machine generated the face or the voice.

Same companies. Same season. Opposite directions.

> Authenticity is what the seller is selling. Efficiency is what the advertiser is buying. So they restrict the machine on one surface and sell it on the other.

Which means the machine is welcome everywhere you spend money and unwelcome everywhere you earn trust.

---

## The Evidence

OpenAI shut down Sora. The video app that was going to eat Hollywood. The Wall Street Journal reported it cost roughly $1 million a day to run. Over the entire life of the product, in-app purchases brought in $2.1 million total. $1 million a day going out. $2.1 million ever coming in.

Disney had signed a three-year licensing deal covering more than 200 characters — Disney, Marvel, Pixar, Star Wars — and was planning to put $1 billion into OpenAI on top of it. Disney walked. The whole thing lasted about six months.

Then, on August 5, Disney signed a deal with TikTok. Same thing they wanted out of Sora. There is a short video feed inside Disney Plus called Verts and they need things to put in it. So hundreds of films and series, characters out of Pixar and Marvel and Star Wars and FX, handed to creators to make short videos with. The videos run on TikTok and inside Disney Plus both.

Put those two deals next to each other. They are eight months apart and they are this whole lesson in miniature. December, $1 billion for a machine to generate the videos. August, a licensing deal for people to make them. Same company. Same characters. Same problem, the same feed to fill. And when the machine version fell over they didn't drop the idea — they moved it to the platform where an actual person is holding the camera.

$1 billion said the machine makes the thing. Eight months later the same company decided a person makes the thing. That is not a technology story. That is the map.

## The Copy Became The Targeting

For about 25 years, picking who saw your ad was a menu. You clicked things. Age 35 to 54. Interested in golf. Bought something in the last ninety days. You picked your people off a list, then wrote some copy to put in front of them, and honestly the copy only had to be good enough not to get you rejected.

That is over on all three major platforms at once.

Google AI Max removed keyword targeting. Gemini reads your landing page and matches it against what the searcher seems to want. Meta put in a new ranking system that reads the ad and the person together rather than scoring every ad on its own. The creative functions as the targeting. OpenAI has no keywords at all. The field is called context hints and it is a free-form list where you write, in plain English, when your thing is worth showing to somebody.

If you can't describe your buyer in words, you can no longer buy the traffic. Not will do worse. Can't. The machine has nothing to match on.

---

## The Thirty Times Gap

Take everybody on a free newsletter list. The typical publisher gets about 0.62% of them to start paying. 6 in 1,000. The best 10% of publishers in finance and investing get 18% to 20%. Nearly two in ten.

That is roughly a thirty times spread, and it is attributed to execution rather than niche. Not the topic, not the size of the list, not the timing. The thirty times is sitting in what you actually do with the thing once you have it.

Making more stuff stopped working, because everybody can make more stuff now. What is left is being specific about who you are for.

---

## The Build/Trust Audit

Break your business into pieces and put every piece on one side of the line. Find where you have a machine sitting where the customer can feel it, and yourself sitting where nobody can see you.

| Piece of your business | Side | What to do about it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Market and competitor research | Build | Machine, hard, no guilt. |
| First drafts of anything | Build | Machine, then rewrite for the tells. |
| Ad targeting and media buying | Build | Machine, fed your Who Document. |
| Subject lines and hook variations | Build | Machine writes fifty, you pick three. |
| Reporting, spreadsheets, admin | Build | Machine. Nobody buys your spreadsheet. |
| The voice in your emails | Trust | You. Don't hand this one over. |
| Your face on a selling video or live | Trust | You. Required on TikTok Shop now. |
| Voiceover on shoppable video | Trust | You. Synthetic voice is banned there. |
| Testimonials and proof | Trust | Real or nothing. |
| Replies to customer email | Trust | You. This is where they decide about you. |
| The promise on the sales page | Trust | You. The ad reviewer reads it hardest. |
| Your name and point of view | Trust | The only thing not getting cheaper. |

---

## Exercise: Audit Your Own Business

Take the table above. Write your own version with your actual assets in it. Find the two places where you have a machine sitting where the customer can feel it. Fix one of them this month. Not both. One.

Run the machine as hard as you can on the build side. Put your actual self on the trust side. And guard the seam between them, because the seam is the words that say who this is for and why they should believe you. That is the one asset both the algorithm and the customer are now demanding from you directly.
