# Foundation Year Reference Video — Production Style Analysis

## Key Production Standards

### Presenter Plates
- Medium close-up, centered, locked-off camera (no movement)
- Shallow depth of field (background heavily blurred)
- Warm lighting, soft and even
- The bob-sweater-library-wide.png plate matches this exact setup (maroon sweater, library bg)

### Slide Design
- Deep navy blue solid backgrounds OR dark moody photographs (lighthouse)
- Pure white primary text
- Muted gold/mustard accent color (thin lines, category headers, price highlights)
- Serif font for headlines (Garamond/Playfair Display style)
- Sans-serif for body copy (Inter/Roboto style)
- Massive negative space
- 2-3-4 column grid layouts
- Editorial navigation bar: "THE WATCH • FOUNDATION YEAR • 01 / 08"
- Thin gold dividing lines between concepts

### Transitions
- ZERO transition effects — all hard straight cuts
- No crossfades, wipes, or pushes
- Text appears with slow smooth opacity fade-in only

### Audio
- NO background music — voice only
- Clean, well-equalized narration
- Silence behind the voice reinforces the "against the noise" theme

### Captions
- NO standard bouncing word-by-word captions
- Text integrated into slides as visual anchors
- White serif font, left-aligned over imagery

### Pacing
- Deliberately slow
- Slides remain 20-40 seconds each
- Presenter segments: opening hook, mid-video personal authority, closing appeal
- Slides carry the bulk of the visual information

### Structure Pattern
- Open with presenter (personal, direct)
- Transition to slides for evidence/framework sections
- Return to presenter for personal testimony moments
- Close with presenter for final appeal
- End on CTA slide
