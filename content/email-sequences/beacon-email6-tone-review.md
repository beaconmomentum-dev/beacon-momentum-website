### Manifesto Tone Comparison

The manifesto's Q&A answer on pricing reads:

> "Beacon Momentum is a community with a membership component. The manifesto is not a sales pitch. It is a statement of values. You can read it, share it, and never give us a dollar, and we consider that a success."

> "If they did not resonate, no hard feelings. The manifesto is free. The Signal articles are free. The clarity is free."

The tone is consistent. Both pieces:
- Present membership as optional, not required
- Frame the free content as sufficient on its own
- Position the paid offering as "going deeper" rather than "getting access"
- Use the gatekeeping metaphor in the same way

### One Recommended Adjustment

**The "$1.40 per day" calculation:**

> "That is less than $1.40 per day for a community, toolset, and framework designed to make you irreplaceable..."

This is a standard sales technique — breaking the annual price into a daily figure to make it feel smaller. It is effective. But it is also the one line in the email that sounds like a sales page rather than a letter from Bob.

The manifesto Q&A does not use this technique. It simply states the price and moves on.

**Options:**
- **Keep it:** The daily breakdown is genuinely useful for someone doing the mental math. It is not dishonest. It is not manipulative. It is just math.
- **Remove it:** The email would read: "Membership is currently $497 per year. If the four emails I sent you resonated..." — cleaner, more consistent with the manifesto's plain-statement approach.

**Recommendation:** Remove the "$1.40 per day" line. The manifesto's approach is to state the price plainly and let the value case (which was built over five preceding emails) do the work. The daily breakdown, while not dishonest, introduces a sales-page register that is inconsistent with the rest of the sequence's tone.

**Revised line:**

> Membership is currently $497 per year.
>
> If the four emails I sent you resonated — if you recognized yourself in the principles — then The Watch is where you build on them.
