**Status:** Recommendation prepared — owner approval required  
**Purpose:** Establish a current, scope-aware market benchmark for the paid Beacon Signal Check before recommending a launch price.  
**Research boundary:** This compares public diagnostic/audit offers. It does not establish a market rate for a service with undefined scope or authorize any public price change.

## Market interpretation

The market separates into at least three different products:

1. **Free or low-cost automated scans:** useful as lead magnets but generally do not include business context, human review, or prioritization.
2. **Bounded human-reviewed diagnostics:** commonly positioned between the automated scan and an agency transformation/audit engagement; the public sources above indicate that this is the most relevant category for Beacon’s prospective paid Signal Check.
3. **Full agency and enterprise audits:** thousands of dollars because they span many pages, data access, campaigns, technical work, stakeholders, competitive research, strategy, and/or implementation support.

The public comparators do **not** support presenting a broad, human-reviewed multi-page audit with a walkthrough at a low-ticket price. The more complete the review becomes—more URLs, competitor work, client data, design/implementation, or live consulting—the more it belongs in the $1,500+ professional-audit market rather than in a lightweight diagnostic product.

## Recommended launch offer

> **Recommendation: launch the paid Beacon Signal Check at $297 USD, but only as a deliberately bounded entry diagnostic—not as a replacement for the full professional audits above.**

The $297 price is appropriate only if Beacon Labs makes the difference between the free and paid offers unmistakable and keeps the paid delivery compact, repeatable, and public-web-based. It is a low-friction first engagement designed to produce prioritized clarity, not a consulting engagement, full audit, technical assessment, or implementation project.

| Component | Free Signal Check | **Paid Beacon Signal Check — $297** |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Self-guided orientation | Human-reviewed priority diagnosis |
| Scope | Public overview; no personalized output | One public domain and up to **three** priority URLs; one named competitor for context |
| Data boundary | No account, intake, or data transfer required | Public-web review only; no analytics, ad-account, CRM, payment, or private-customer access |
| Deliverable | On-page results or short framework | Five-to-seven page written brief with up to seven prioritized findings and a 30/60/90-day sequence |
| Walkthrough | None | Up to 15-minute recorded walkthrough; no live consulting is included |
| Turnaround | Immediate | Up to five business days after complete intake |
| Explicit exclusions | Personalized analysis, report, roadmap, implementation | Design files, copy rewrite, code, technical remediation, access-based analysis, campaign management, guarantees, and implementation work |

### Why this price is defensible

1. It sits above an automated lead magnet and requires the human judgment, prioritization, and roadmap that the market associates with paid work.[1]
2. It remains visibly narrower than the $1,540–$2,300+ human-reviewed offers and materially below the $1,750+ design-inclusive audit category.[4][5][6]
3. It preserves a clear upgrade boundary: a request for more than three URLs, more than one competitor, client credentials/data, live strategy time, design, code, or implementation is **not** a $297 Signal Check and must be separately scoped rather than silently absorbed.

### Re-pricing triggers

The $297 launch price should be re-tested after the first 10 completed reports and reconsidered if any of the following occurs:

- delivery regularly exceeds 90 minutes of review, writing, and walkthrough time per report;
- prospects expect implementation, paid-media, analytics, CRM, legal/compliance, or private-data analysis;
- more than one follow-up discussion is needed to explain the report;
- the conversion rate from paid Signal Check to a separately scoped service is too low to justify the delivery effort; or
- Beacon changes the scope to the original larger version: five URLs, two competitors, 8–12 findings, and a 30-minute live walkthrough.

For the original broader scope, the market evidence indicates a substantially higher professional-service price is warranted; it should not be launched at $297 without narrowing the promise.

Approve or amend the **$297 bounded Beacon Signal Check** described above before any public Labs copy, CTA, payment, GHL attribution, or fulfillment workflow is implemented. This is market research and scope analysis, not a forecast of sales or profitability.
