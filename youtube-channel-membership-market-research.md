# YouTube Channel Memberships: Current Market and Platform Research

**Research date:** August 5, 2026

YouTube’s United States pricing list includes monthly membership levels from **$0.99** through **$499.99**, though available levels and local customer pricing vary by region. YouTube recommends starting with one to three levels and cautions creators to choose the lowest price carefully because existing members retain their original price after price changes.[1] [2]

Channel memberships can offer badges, members-only posts, videos, Shorts, live streams, chat access, and early access. YouTube recommends low-effort, sustainable benefits and says creators keep 70% of membership revenue after taxes and fees.[3]

## Beacon recommendation

Launch only after a reliable free publishing rhythm and an eight-week member-content backlog. Begin with one support-first tier—not a replacement for The Watch—with a member badge, occasional members-only field note, early access to selected public videos, and a monthly recorded context note. Do not include investment claims, services, guaranteed outcomes, consulting, or direct founder time.
