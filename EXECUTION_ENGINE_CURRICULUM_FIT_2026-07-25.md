# Execution Engine Curriculum Fit Assessment

**Verdict: YES.** The curriculum can become a productive part of the current Beacon offering, but it belongs **inside Beacon Venture** as an edited member curriculum asset—not as a separate $197/$297 standalone product or external checkout funnel.

> **Recommended role:** “Venture Execution Sprint” — a six-lesson practical sequence that helps a Watch member move from a validated idea to a small, working, AI-assisted offer or prototype.

## Why Beacon Venture Is the Correct Home

Beacon Venture already owns the journey from validation through launch, growth, and systematization. Its current curriculum covers digital product architecture, AI-assisted operations, limited drops that prove demand before scale, and resilient income creation. The Execution Engine’s strongest material—AI-enabled prototyping, a 48-hour build walkthrough, and output-scaling systems—fits that journey directly.

The other pillars can borrow selected tools, but none offers the same coherent product-level home. Beacon Work focuses on professional value and workplace adaptation; Beacon Systems focuses on organizational infrastructure and client operations; Beacon Life supports personal stabilization and capacity; Beacon Labs documents research and proof. Making the course a shared or standalone product would dilute those boundaries.

| Existing Execution Engine asset | Fit in Beacon Venture | Required edit |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The Execution Gap** | **Foundation lesson** before Venture validation. | Replace the unsupported “neurological” framing with an evidence-aware review of planning friction, avoidance, and decision bottlenecks. Tie the exercise to one defined venture outcome. |
| **2. The Identity Shift** | **Supporting lesson** for founder operating habits. | Reframe from a broad “rewiring” promise to an operator identity practice: commitments, shipping cadence, and post-mortem reflection. |
| **3. The 5-Day Discipline Protocol** | **Venture operating sprint**. | Rename to a five-day shipping cadence. Replace permanence claims with a repeatable weekly practice and measurable checkpoints. |
| **4. The Vibe Coding Method** | **Core practical lesson** within the Launch stage. | Reframe as AI-assisted prototyping for non-technical founders; add demand validation, privacy/security boundaries, basic QA, and a decision tree for when not to build software. |
| **5. Your First 48-Hour Build** | **Core capstone** in the Launch stage. | Make it a guided prototype-to-offer sprint, beginning with proof of demand. Avoid implying that every idea should become a software product. |
| **6. Scaling the Engine** | **Systematize-stage lesson**. | Focus on documented workflows, customer delivery, delegation readiness, and tools the founder controls; remove vague output-multiplication promises. |
| **Prompt library, planners, and QC checklists** | **Venture member resource library**. | Audit every prompt and checklist, brand them to Beacon Venture, and align them with the Watch’s actual implementation and support boundaries. |
| **Execution Gap Assessment** | **Optional Venture onboarding diagnostic**. | Convert it into an internal Pathfinder supplement rather than a separate client-facing or team-facing assessment. |

## Required Product Decisions

The course should be integrated as part of **The Watch’s $497/year annual membership**, not sold alongside it. The member-facing promise should be a practical Venture pathway asset, not “instant access,” a countdown offer, or a substitute for community and accountability.

The recommended sequence is: **Validate → Venture Execution Sprint → Launch → Systematize**. The current 48-hour build material should appear only after a member has identified a real problem, a target audience, and a minimum viable offer. That preserves Beacon Venture’s stated doctrine: prove demand before building.

## Editorial and Compliance Guardrails

The curriculum needs a full edit before reuse. Remove urgency timers, early-bird pricing, broad psychological or neurological claims, “permanent shift” language, and any unsupported promise of instant outcomes. Retain the practical delivery: prompts, planners, a build walkthrough, and operational checklists. Make clear that the material is educational, that software is not always the right vehicle, and that the member remains responsible for testing, privacy, security, and business decisions.

## Recommended Disposition of the Existing Funnel

**Do not keep the current external offer page live as the curriculum’s sales page.** Retire or redirect it to the Beacon pricing page while the asset is edited and integrated into Beacon Venture. A future standalone offer could be revisited only after it has a defined audience, distinct outcome, proven fulfillment path, and a non-conflicting place in the product ladder.
