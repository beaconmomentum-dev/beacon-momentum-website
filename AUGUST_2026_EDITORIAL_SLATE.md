## Editorial Boundaries

The articles use the public Signal to explain practical operating judgment. They may guide readers to **The Watch** for deeper operating dossiers or to **Beacon Labs** only when a B2B service question is explicit. They must not imply investment returns, financial advice, diagnostic outcomes, client-specific consulting, automated decision-making, or privileged access.

Each release uses an existing approved 1920 × 1080 derivative from the Beacon editorial library. Future original art must meet `BEACON_EDITORIAL_IMAGE_SPECIFICATION.md`: full-content preservation, documented source and rights, descriptive alt text, and no baked-in playback icon. Audio controls, where a narration file exists, remain on the article route only.

## Source and Review Method

Each article will cite its relevant source within the article body and identify the Beacon control or workflow it translates. The source-grounded brief checker must be run before publication for any article that describes a third-party incident, market development, government action, or company claim.
