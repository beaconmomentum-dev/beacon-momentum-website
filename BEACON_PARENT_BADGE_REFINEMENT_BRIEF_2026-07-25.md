# Beacon Momentum, LLC — Parent Badge Refinement Brief

**Status:** Candidate concepts generated; parent-brand approval pending  
**Prepared:** July 25, 2026  
**Decision linked to:** Brand System Phase 0, D-06 — Shared visual foundation

## Purpose

The supplied lighthouse artwork confirms a strong, recognizable Beacon family: **deep maritime navy, a warm purposeful lantern, and a directional beam**. It should remain valuable as editorial illustration and larger-format visual language. The existing circular badge, however, needs a more disciplined color relationship and a simpler presentation before it is adopted as a parent-system mark.

> **Badge rule:** The parent badge must communicate “Beacon” at small size before it communicates any tagline, architectural texture, or campaign-style illustration.

## Assessment of the current asset family

| Asset role | What to retain | What to change before system adoption |
|---|---|---|
| Architectural lighthouse illustration | Strong tower silhouette, navy structure, warm lantern, clear rightward light | Keep it for hero/editorial use; do not compress its masonry, railing, or detailed beam into a small badge. |
| Aspirational lighthouse illustration | More polished navy-and-gold relationship and direct visual focus | Treat it as campaign/section art, not the primary identity mark. |
| Current circular badge | Circular container, lighthouse metaphor, parent-company meaning | Remove the oversized circular text from the small-size mark; replace olive/green cast and edge contamination; reduce visual complexity; separate the wordmark/tagline from the emblem. |
| Clean lighthouse cutout | Isolated subject and transparency intent | Eliminate checkerboard or green-fringe artifacts in every production export; use it only where a full illustration is appropriate. |

## Approved color direction for review

The new badge should express a stable **navy → signal gold → restrained sea-glass** hierarchy. Signal gold carries illumination and emphasis; it must never read as a fluorescent yellow or substitute for body text. Sea-glass is secondary and should never appear as a lime edge, halo, or uncontrolled green spill.

| Token role | Direction | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| **Beacon Ink** | Near-black maritime navy | Default dark field, structural lines, monochrome mark |
| **Beacon Deep Navy** | Saturated blue-navy | Core badge field and lighthouse body |
| **Signal Gold** | Warm, slightly muted maritime gold | Lantern, directional beam, small emphasis, premium cue |
| **Harbor Teal** | Restrained sea-glass teal | Optional micro-accent only; never the dominant parent mark color |
| **Paper/Mist** | Warm light neutral | Light-background use and contrast field |

## Presentation system

The badge should be deployed as a system rather than as a single image used at every size.

| Variant | Job | Required composition |
|---|---|---|
| **Parent signet** | Header mark, favicon, app icon, social-avatar base | Wordless, compact lighthouse/light symbol with a clean silhouette and transparent or solid navy field. |
| **Parent badge** | Footer, certificate, editorial stamp, larger social use | Circular or shield container permitted; no mandatory curved tagline. |
| **Wordmark lockup** | Site header, legal/footer identity | Parent signet placed beside separately set typography. The name must not depend on generated badge lettering. |
| **Property badge** | Community, Trading, Labs, and related-app route confirmation | Parent signet remains recognizable; a disciplined property accent or small device differentiates the property without replacing the parent mark. |
| **Illustration family** | Hero, pathway, campaign, and explanatory art | Full architectural lighthouse and broader beam remain allowed, but never serve as the minimum-size icon. |

## Approval criteria

The selected direction should pass all of the following before it becomes an official shared asset.

- The lighthouse/light silhouette is recognizable at 24 pixels without the word “Beacon.”
- The mark remains legible on both deep navy and light-paper backgrounds.
- The parent mark does not use fluorescent green, olive haze, thin raster edges, visual noise, or checkerboard remnants.
- Gold is reserved for the lantern/beam and small emphasis, maintaining sufficient contrast in all interface contexts.
- The symbol can be reproduced in one color for accessibility, printing, or low-fidelity contexts.
- Property-level accents differentiate Community, Trading, Labs, and the related app without obscuring the parent relationship.
- The final production export includes transparent PNG assets and a compact favicon/app-icon variant.

## Decision request

Select **A**, **B**, **C**, or request a focused revision. The recommended default is **A — Beacon Roundel** because it connects most directly to the existing badge while correcting the color, text-density, and small-size weaknesses. Once selected, the chosen mark will be converted into the Brand System asset hierarchy and recorded as part of the Phase 0 visual-foundation approval.
