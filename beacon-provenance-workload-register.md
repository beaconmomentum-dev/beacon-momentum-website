# Beacon Provenance and Workload Register

**Status:** Initial operational register for founder review. This is a governance tool, not a public promise.

| Workflow | Retained evidence and operating memory | Permitted external dependency | Human approval gate | Minimum fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Source-grounded field brief | Source register, saved primary-source excerpts/citations, research notes, claim ledger, approved draft, publication date. | Current public research, licensed data, bounded drafting assistance. | Named publisher verifies claims, sources, citations, and headline. | Publish only verified core explanation, or pause. |
| Foundation Year communication | Claims ledger, founder script, ledger PDF, press assets, captions/transcript, versioned public copy. | Press distribution, video hosting, accessibility tooling after final approval. | Founder approves spoken claims, figures, destination, captions, and support invitation. | Keep press-review placeholder live; do not release unfinished video or new payment message. |
| Member and community editorial production | Editorial calendar, approved resource list, attribution record, terms, member-release log, change notes. | YouTube/distribution, research, moderated communications. | Editorial owner confirms the resource matches stated membership promise and avoids founder-access or outcome claims. | Deliver defined resource asynchronously, communicate delay, preserve terms. |

## Operating rules

1. A model output is never the final authority for a public claim, membership promise, payment message, or customer outcome.
2. Credentials, client secrets, raw payment data, confidential customer information, and protected source material do not belong in general-purpose prompts or public channels.
3. Preserve the source trail needed to explain a decision; do not retain data simply because a system can store it.
4. Use local or controlled systems for durable working memory where practical. Use external tools for bounded capability, retrieval, delivery, or redundancy.
5. When in doubt, pause the workflow and return it to a human reviewer.
