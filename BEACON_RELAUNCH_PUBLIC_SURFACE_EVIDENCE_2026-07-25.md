# Beacon Relaunch: Public-Surface Evidence Notes

**Purpose:** Preserve factual observations from read-only public-site inspection for the relaunch readiness program. These are not approvals of the observed claims, testimonials, prices, policy posture, or delivery arrangements.

## Hollow Threads — `hollowthreads.store`

| Area | Observation | Relaunch implication |
|---|---|---|
| Role | The live site presents Hollow Threads as a made-to-order emo/grunge apparel storefront organized around “Emotional Frequencies.” | It is a distinct independent-commerce surface, consistent with the parent architecture’s high-level role. |
| Commercial claims | The public page displays product prices beginning at `$29.99`, free shipping at `$50+`, made-to-order production in `3–5` business days, 30-day returns, size availability, secure-checkout wording, an email capture, social links, and a chat control. | Verify actual store operator, payment processor, fulfillment provider, shipping terms, returns policy, privacy policy, customer-support route, inventory/production claims, taxes, and consent before any new Beacon referral or cross-promotion. |
| Cross-property boundary | The inspected public page does not visibly state that it is a Beacon Momentum product or a shared account/data environment. | Retain independent-commerce framing; do not imply member discounts, shared accounts, shared checkout, shared customer data, or parent-company fulfillment without a documented agreement and disclosure. |
| Status | **P1—independent commerce verification required.** | It may remain a clearly labeled external portfolio/referral link only after the parent link registry’s disclosure and data-separation rules are applied. |

## Digital Grandpa — `digitalgrandpa.org`

| Area | Observation | Relaunch implication |
|---|---|---|
| Role | The live site presents a veteran-owned legacy/mission surface centered on personal rebuilding, free resources, books/tools, blog content, and the “Rise & Reclaim” community. | Its public role requires a separately defined parent-company relationship, audience, and referral boundary before relaunch alignment. |
| High-sensitivity subject matter | Public language addresses veterans, formerly incarcerated people, at-risk youth, personal growth, mental/emotional topics, recovery-style framing, employment, financial and investment-adjacent matters, and community support. | Do not cross-promote through general Beacon membership or B2B pathways until counsel/owner review defines appropriate disclosures, safeguarding, referral, moderation, and crisis/escalation boundaries. |
| Public claims | The page contains veteran/status assertions, personal-history statements, recognition claims, aggregate outcome-style statistics, and testimonial-style narratives. | Treat all as **unverified claims pending evidence review**. Do not repeat, syndicate, or use them in Beacon marketing, structured data, or referrals until sources, permissions, and appropriate presentation are confirmed. |
| Commerce/community | The inspected surface promotes “Rise & Reclaim” but the public inspect did not establish account ownership, price, payment route, terms, privacy, support, or service-delivery controls. | No new CTA, price, donation, membership, or shared-data assertion is authorized before an operational inventory and policy review. |
| Status | **P0—high-sensitivity claims, audience, and policy review required.** | Isolate from the general Beacon relaunch until role, owners, policies, evidence, and safe public handoffs are approved. |
