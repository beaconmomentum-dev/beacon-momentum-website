# Beacon Merchandise — V1 Decision Record

**Decision status:** Owner-approved launch scope; palette and commerce operations remain pending formal release review  
**Decision date:** July 25, 2026  
**Decision owner:** Bob, Beacon Momentum LLC  
**Applies to:** Beacon public merchandise page and future fulfillment configuration

> **Decision:** Launch a focused, ordinary-commerce Beacon merchandise collection consisting of a Supporter Edition T-shirt, a Signal Journal/Field Notebook, one drinkware item (Coastline Tumbler or Water Bottle, selected during fulfillment validation), and a Beacon cap/hat. The crewneck and Beacon Roundel medallion or field card are validation-gated later extensions.

## 1. Approved launch taxonomy

| Product role | Approved V1 item | Scope rule | Not authorized by this record |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flagship affiliation** | Supporter Edition T-shirt | Retain the existing `$100` product concept pending final unit-economics and fulfillment validation. | Broad apparel line, variable fundraising claims, or unverified scarcity language. |
| **Practice and reflection** | Signal Journal / Field Notebook | A practical navigation-oriented writing object aligned with Beacon’s learning system. | A claim that the item changes outcomes or guarantees a result. |
| **Daily-use utility** | Coastline Tumbler **or** Water Bottle | Select one drinkware form factor after supplier, shipping, durability, and gross-margin validation. | Launching multiple nearly identical drinkware SKUs without a fulfillment rationale. |
| **Visible affiliation** | Beacon cap / hat | Use one deliberate silhouette and a restrained Roundel application. | A broad hat, headwear, or colorway catalog at launch. |
| **Deferred extension** | Crewneck; Beacon Roundel medallion or field card | Evaluate only after the launch collection has real demand, fulfillment, and return evidence. | Treating them as approved initial inventory. |

## 2. Required marketplace language

The merchandise collection is an **ordinary retail offering**. Its approved purpose note is:

> “Net proceeds from this collection support the continued development of the Beacon ecosystem and resources for its members.”

The product page and checkout must not use **donation**, **tax-deductible**, **scholarship contribution**, **raffle**, or charitable-allocation language. No representation about allocations, beneficiaries, or tax treatment may be added without qualified review and a separately approved operating model.

## 3. Provisional palette direction — pending D-06 approval

The following is a merchandise-specific application proposal, not a replacement for the pending Beacon Brand System token decision. It creates a restrained physical palette suitable for the approved Roundel pair and avoids a generic promotional assortment.

| Palette role | Proposed treatment | Recommended use | Control rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Beacon Navy** | Deep maritime navy | Base T-shirt, cap, and dark drinkware surfaces | Pair with the **Inverse Roundel** only. |
| **Beacon Canvas** | Warm natural/off-white | Journal cover, T-shirt alternative, packaging and product inserts | Pair with the **Light Roundel** only. |
| **Signal Gold** | Reserved brass/gold accent | Small stitch, foil, trim, or detail line | Never use as a dominant full-garment field. |
| **Harbor Gray** | Soft neutral gray | Secondary cap, notebook interior, product photography support | Use only where contrast and mark legibility are verified. |

The intent is a compact **Navy / Canvas / Gold / Gray** assortment with one dominant core color per item rather than many colorways. Final values, accessible digital equivalents, embroidery/print specifications, and supplier-ready artwork remain subject to D-06 approval and production sampling.

## 4. Remaining release gates

| Gate | Required evidence before sale or fulfillment | Owner / reviewer |
|---|---|---|
| **Brand-system approval** | D-06 decision on shared semantic tokens, typography, badge logic, property accents, and an accountable systems owner | Beacon Momentum LLC owner + brand systems owner |
| **Commerce route** | Storefront, fulfillment, shipping geography/costs, taxes, returns/refunds, privacy, and support ownership | Commerce/operations owner + policy reviewer |
| **Product economics** | Supplier quote, landed unit cost, price, gross-margin target, inventory model, MOQ, and contingency plan | Commerce/operations owner |
| **Claims and disclosures** | Approved product descriptions, purpose note, non-charitable language, customer-data boundary, and relevant policies | Policy reviewer + parent offer owner |
| **Quality control** | Physical sample, embroidery/print quality review, Roundel legibility, packaging check, and product-page/accessibility QA | Brand systems + operations owners |

## 5. Evidence basis

The selection was informed by a bounded comparison of official merchandise catalogs from related education, creator-community, personal-development, and purpose-led brands. The evidence indicates the relevant role families are daily-use utility, visible affiliation, ritual/symbol objects, and practical learning aids—not a broad generic merchandise store.[1]
