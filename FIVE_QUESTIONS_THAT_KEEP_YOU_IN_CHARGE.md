# Five Questions That Keep You in Charge

## Purpose

This public Beacon field note gives individuals and small teams a simple way to decide whether an AI-assisted workflow is ready for responsible use. It is educational material, not cybersecurity, legal, compliance, or financial advice.

## The five questions

1. **What exact job is this workflow allowed to do?**
2. **What systems, data, and credentials can it reach?**
3. **What is explicitly off limits?**
4. **What record will show what happened and what it cost?**
5. **Which named human still owns the final decision?**

## Beacon principle

> The question is not whether an AI can act. The question is whether you can explain what it was allowed to do, what it did, what it cost, and who remained accountable.
