## Guardrail observation

The archived concept combines consideration (the $100 ticket book), chance (a stated winning probability), and a prize (an RV). In many jurisdictions, that combination is regulated as a raffle or lottery. The actual compliance status depends on jurisdiction, sponsoring entity, charitable status, registration, official rules, eligibility, no-purchase alternatives where required, bonded/escrow arrangements, prize title, tax reporting, and consumer-disclosure requirements. No public launch or revival should occur until a qualified attorney and the appropriate financial/tax professionals review the final structure.

> **Current operating conclusion:** The legacy RV page is not active at its historic public destination. Treat it as a retired historical concept pending a documented decision, rather than as a current fundraising product.

## Questions required before comparing or reviving an RV initiative

1. What was the intended sponsoring entity, jurisdiction, and charitable or beneficiary structure?
2. Was an RV ever purchased, committed, donated, titled, insured, or otherwise controlled for the effort?
3. Was the purpose a raffle, sweepstakes, donor campaign, merchandise incentive, or another model?
4. Were official rules, legal review, state registrations, financial controls, and recipient-selection criteria ever completed?
5. Does the new scholarship objective replace the RV concept, coexist with it as a separate initiative, or use it only as historical research?
