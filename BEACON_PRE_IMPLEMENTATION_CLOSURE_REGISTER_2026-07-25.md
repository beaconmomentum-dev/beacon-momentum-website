The membership source of truth remains **partially blocked** until the approved request-to-payment-to-Community activation chain is implemented and verified, and Beacon Community’s inconsistent public CTA is corrected. Beacon Labs remains blocked until the approved $297 offer has controlled intake, payment, consent, delivery, and policy evidence.
