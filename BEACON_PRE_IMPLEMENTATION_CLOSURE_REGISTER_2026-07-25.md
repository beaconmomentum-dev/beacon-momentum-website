## 5. Phase 0 exit checklist

Phase 0 is complete only when every item below is true.

- [ ] One approved Beacon Community membership offer has an owner, price/term, enrollment destination, policy link, and support path.
- [ ] The Execution Engine has one approved, non-conflicting disposition and no unmanaged public sales path.
- [ ] Beacon Labs has one approved diagnostic ladder, documented data/payment flow, required policy surfaces, and verified payment-gated paid intake.
- [ ] Beacon Trading has working current pricing/legal routes and an adopted simulation-only content standard.
- [ ] Beacon Community has a documented route, safety, moderation, privacy, and support-escalation posture.
- [ ] The related app has an authorized technical/data inventory and an approved role decision.
- [ ] The brand token, typography, mark/badge, and property-accent foundation is approved.
- [ ] The offer, claims, and link ledgers have named owners, source material, review dates, and release-gate status.
- [ ] No cross-property CTA transfers personal data, auto-creates accounts, pre-fills intake, shares tags, or merges audience records.
