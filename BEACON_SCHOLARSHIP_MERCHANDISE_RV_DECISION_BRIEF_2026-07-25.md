# Beacon Momentum — Scholarship, Supporter Merchandise, and RV Concept Decision Brief

**Status:** Superseded for launch design by the simplified standard merchandise-page direction, July 25, 2026  
**Prepared:** July 25, 2026  
**Decision owner:** Beacon Momentum LLC  
**Scope:** A potential scholarship initiative for members rebuilding their lives; a premium supporter T-shirt and limited merchandise program; and the prior RV raffle concept.

> **Important:** This is a strategy and operating brief, not legal, tax, charitable-solicitation, raffle, gaming, or financial advice. A qualified attorney and tax/accounting professional should review the final legal entity, public language, scholarship rules, accounting treatment, and any charitable or prize-related claims before launch.

## Superseding owner direction — simple Beacon merchandise page

The owner has chosen not to create a separate scholarship fund, fundraiser, restricted allocation, raffle, or award-selection program at this stage. The active direction is a straightforward **Beacon merchandise page** that may begin with a premium $100 Beacon Supporter Edition T-shirt and expand only through deliberate product decisions.

The working public note is:

> **Net proceeds from this collection support the continued development of the Beacon ecosystem and resources for its members.**

This is ordinary commerce, not a donation, tax-deductible contribution, scholarship award, membership purchase, prize entry, or investment. The legacy RV raffle remains retired. The historical analysis below is retained only as context for why the more complex path is not being adopted now.

## Executive judgment

**Proceed with a tightly governed, dignity-first scholarship pilot funded by one premium supporter product. Do not launch a broad merchandise catalog at the same time. Do not revive the RV raffle.**

The recommended initial product is a limited-run **Beacon Supporter Edition** T-shirt priced at **$100**, but the public promise must be precise: it is a product purchase that funds a disclosed contribution to a scholarship initiative. Unless the legal and tax structure expressly supports it, it must **not** be described as a tax-deductible donation, charity, raffle ticket, investment, or guarantee of aid.

The member-facing initiative should be positioned as the **Beacon Restart Scholarship Initiative** (working name): a transparent, time-bounded access-support program for qualifying Beacon Community members who are rebuilding stability. The program should be funded through a documented internal allocation—not vague “proceeds” language—and administered separately from The Watch membership sales record.

## Recommended operating model

### 1. Program structure

Use a **separate internal program ledger** under Beacon Momentum LLC until legal and tax professionals advise otherwise. The public program should have a named operating owner and a written purpose statement. The underlying cash allocation, scholarship pool, applications, awards, reversals, and remaining balance must be traceable without relying on marketing language.

The first published version should define only what is known and controllable:

| Field | Recommended pilot standard |
|---|---|
| **Working program name** | Beacon Restart Scholarship Initiative |
| **Purpose** | Help qualifying Beacon Community members access or retain a defined, approved Beacon benefit while rebuilding stability. |
| **Sponsor/policy owner** | Beacon Momentum LLC |
| **Support path** | `support@beaconmomentum.com` |
| **Eligibility** | Draft only after owner approval; use objective, private criteria rather than public hardship narratives. |
| **Award type** | A defined, non-cash access-support benefit—not a promise of cash, investment return, or financial advice. |
| **Selection** | At least two reviewers, documented conflict recusal, and a short written rationale for every award decision. |
| **Reporting** | Aggregate reporting only: funds allocated, awards made, awards pending, and remaining available balance. No recipient names, stories, or sensitive circumstances without separate informed consent. |

### 2. Product and allocation design

Launch one limited supporter item before a catalog. The $100 price can work if the product has genuine physical value and the allocation is explicit.

> **Preferred public language:** “Each paid Beacon Supporter Edition shirt includes a disclosed $X contribution to the Beacon Restart Scholarship Initiative, administered by Beacon Momentum LLC under the published program rules.”

Do **not** use “100% of proceeds” unless legal/accounting review confirms exactly what counts as proceeds and all cost, tax, payment, fulfillment, refund, and chargeback outcomes are addressed. A fixed dollar amount per completed, non-refunded order is easier for a supporter to understand and for the team to reconcile.

The supporter item should be a **limited edition**, not a product catalogue placeholder. It should use the approved Roundel system with restrained, premium creative direction and no language that exploits member hardship.

### 3. GHL, data, and source-of-truth boundaries

GHL may label revenue streams for lifetime-value analysis, but the scholarship and merchandise activity must not automatically create, upgrade, downgrade, or expose a Beacon Community member account. Use separate internal attribution labels, for example:

| Revenue/data event | Required handling |
|---|---|
| The Watch membership | Membership-source tag; retain Beacon Community as the membership system of record. |
| Supporter shirt purchase | Separate merchandise/scholarship-supporter tag; no implied membership benefit. |
| Scholarship application | Separate restricted-access record with a stated purpose, minimum necessary data, retention schedule, and no automatic marketing audience merge. |
| Award decision | Confidential program record; aggregate status only for reporting. |

No public page should imply that a supporter’s purchase buys membership, preference in scholarship selection, tax deductibility, a prize chance, or financial return.

## 90-day pilot sequence

| Period | Deliverable | Release gate |
|---|---|---|
| **Days 0–30 — Foundation** | Program purpose, legal/tax review request, named owner, eligibility/rubric, reviewer/conflict policy, allocation formula, refund/chargeback policy, accounting class, privacy/retention record, and vendor choice. | No product page or presale until the public statements match the approved operating model. |
| **Days 31–60 — Product preparation** | Final shirt design, vendor proof, fulfillment/returns terms, supporter page copy, allocation disclosure, two-reviewer test workflow, GHL tagging map, and aggregate reporting template. | Complete an end-to-end internal test: order → allocation → refund scenario → scholarship record → aggregate report. |
| **Days 61–90 — Limited pilot** | Limited run, weekly reconciliation, private award review, support handling, and a post-pilot operating review. | No catalog expansion until the ledger balances, the fulfillment experience is acceptable, and the owner reviews the transparency report. |

## Minimum launch controls

1. **Named owner:** Beacon Momentum LLC names the operating and financial-reconciliation owner.
2. **Written public rules:** Purpose, price, allocation, eligibility, selection process, support contact, fulfillment/returns, privacy, and reporting cadence are public before launch.
3. **Two-person award control:** No sole reviewer decides awards or overrides eligibility without documented recusal and approval.
4. **Separate ledgers:** The Watch membership revenue, general merchandise revenue, supporter-item allocation, and scholarship awards are distinct records.
5. **No private data leakage:** Scholarship applicants are not added to sales audiences or shared across properties without a documented, lawful basis and clear notice.
6. **No misleading labels:** No raffle, chance, donation tax deduction, charity, “100% proceeds,” or recipient guarantee language without professional review and verified mechanics.
7. **Aggregate accountability:** Publish a periodic program summary that never identifies applicants or recipients without explicit consent.

## RV raffle decision

**Recommendation: retire the RV raffle as a live commercial concept.** Preserve the source record as historical research only. It should not be resurrected through SamCart, GHL, merchandise, social, email, or new Beacon public pages.

If a future owner wishes to revisit any prize-based promotion, the first step is not creative, checkout, ticket sales, or a landing page. It is a documented fact inventory and qualified legal/tax review of the jurisdiction, sponsor, prize ownership, official rules, licensing/registration, insurance, eligibility, alternative entry requirements where applicable, disclosures, payment handling, and reporting obligations.
