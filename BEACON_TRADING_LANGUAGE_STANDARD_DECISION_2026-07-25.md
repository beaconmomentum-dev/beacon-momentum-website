# Beacon Trading — Language Standard Decision Record

**Decision status:** Owner-approved  
**Decision date:** July 25, 2026  
**Decision owner:** Bob, Beacon Momentum LLC  
**Applies to:** Beacon Trading homepage, guides, dashboard, pricing, policy, support, and all linked public-copy surfaces

> **Decision:** Beacon Trading is an **education-first, simulation-only practice environment**. It does not provide investment advice, brokerage, custody, trade execution, live signals, managed accounts, or performance guarantees.

## 1. Approved vocabulary

| Communication purpose | Approved wording examples |
|---|---|
| Describe the product role | Financial education; simulated practice; learning environment; scenario analysis; educational tools; practice workflows. |
| Describe user activity | Explore scenarios; learn concepts; practice process; review examples; build familiarity with the platform’s educational materials. |
| Describe access and boundaries | Educational access; simulated environment; no investment advice; no brokerage or custody; no trade-execution service. |

## 2. Prohibited or restricted vocabulary

| Do not use | Why it is prohibited in Beacon Trading public copy |
|---|---|
| Execute trades; trade execution; live trading | Implies execution capability or a live trading service. |
| Live signals; buy/sell calls; alerts that direct action | Implies actionable recommendations or an advisory-like service. |
| Brokerage; custody; managed accounts | Implies regulated service functions that are outside the approved role. |
| Guaranteed returns; performance promises; outcome claims | Creates a financial-results implication incompatible with the education-and-simulation boundary. |

## 3. Implementation requirements

This decision is an approved policy direction, not evidence that every Beacon Trading surface is already compliant. Source/CMS access, route verification, and policy review are still required before any public copy, pricing, dashboard, guide, or legal-page change is published. The implementation must retain appropriate proximity disclosures near relevant actions and must not introduce an implied connection to Beacon Community membership, member accounts, brokerage functions, or financial advice.

## 4. Closure evidence required

| Requirement | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Content remediation | Copy diff or published-page evidence showing the approved vocabulary on the homepage, guides, dashboard, and pricing surface. |
| Policy and route review | Verified Terms, Privacy, Disclaimer, No Investment Advice, purchase/access, refund, and pricing-route status. |
| Source authorization | Authorized source/CMS access and a named Beacon Trading owner or policy reviewer. |
| Public validation | Route/link test and screenshot record demonstrating disclaimer proximity and removal of prohibited execution-oriented language. |
