# Beacon Cross-Property Relaunch Implementation Program

**Status:** Active program plan; no wave authorizes public relaunch or traffic expansion until its exit gate is passed.
**Parent owner:** Bob / Beacon Momentum, LLC
**Program purpose:** Align the public front door, membership/community surface, education/simulation surface, B2B services, related app, and affiliated properties around verified roles, offers, routes, policies, data boundaries, and a shared brand foundation.

> **Relaunch principle.** A common visual language cannot correct a contradictory product role, price, route, policy, account, payment, or data flow. Resolve the system first; then apply shared design components to the correctly governed surfaces.

## 1. Current program position

The ecosystem has a usable parent-company offer, claims, and link ledger and a public-site design foundation. It does **not** yet have a relaunch-ready set of public properties. Current evidence identifies P0 conflicts in Beacon Community, Beacon Trading, Beacon Labs, and the related app/customer-data hub. It also identifies high-sensitivity or independent-commerce gates for Digital Grandpa and Hollow Threads.[1] [2] [3]

| Program outcome | Current state |
|---|---|
| One verified public front door | **Partial.** `beaconmomentum.com` has a current `$497/year` Watch offer, but several additional offers and external destinations require source-of-truth review. |
| One verified membership path | **Not established.** The live `beaconcommunity.net` identity, offer, and account model diverge from the canonical Watch architecture. |
| One verified education/simulation destination | **Not established.** Beacon Trading has a documented simulation boundary but conflicting public prices and domain/deployment references. |
| One verified B2B services path | **Not established.** Beacon Labs has controlled Signal Check intake evidence, but the live public surface conflicts with approved scope, route, price, and fulfillment state. |
| One approved related-app role | **Not established.** The live app makes incompatible membership, health, financial, and account claims while the candidate customer-data source has unresolved data-governance risk. |
| Governed affiliate/referral posture | **Partial.** Hollow Threads appears to be separate commerce; Digital Grandpa is a high-sensitivity mission surface. Neither may receive casual cross-promotion. |

## 2. Non-negotiable program rules

| Rule | Implementation consequence |
|---|---|
| **No source-of-truth conflict may be hidden by design.** | A route or card is removed, held, or labeled pending—not visually normalized—when its price, role, policy, or data boundary conflicts with the parent ledger. |
| **Every cross-property link is an explicit referral unless approved otherwise.** | A handoff identifies the destination, destination role, and absence of automatic account/data transfer. |
| **No traffic expansion while a destination is P0.** | Do not add paid media, new homepage hero placement, newsletter promotion, affiliate promotion, or SEO amplification to a P0 surface. |
| **No public claim without a verifiable record.** | Prices, outcomes, testimonials, client logos, security claims, savings, program statistics, and availability claims need their own approved evidence. |
| **No cross-property identity or data inference.** | Do not prefill forms, create accounts, merge CRM records, share member status, share payment data, or imply one login unless a documented consent and data-flow decision authorizes it. |
| **No sensitive or regulated adjacency without dedicated review.** | Financial education/simulation, health/recovery-adjacent content, B2B services, client data, and AI agents require proximity disclosures, ownership, escalation, privacy, and human-review controls. |

## 4. Implementation waves

No wave may begin until its dependency conditions are met. A “documentation complete” status does not equal “production ready.”

| Wave | Scope | Work allowed | Work explicitly prohibited | Exit gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Freeze and source control** | All public properties | Record production domains, source repositories, deployment branch/host, named owner, policy route, analytics owner, payment/CRM boundary, and last verification date. Remove only confirmed broken/unsafe public links with owner approval. | New offer cards, pricing, paid traffic, cross-site account claims, data integration, or broad visual redesign. | Each property has a verified source/deployment/owner record or is marked unavailable/held. |
| **1. P0 decisions and containment** | Community, related app, Trading, Labs, Execution Engine | Execute D-01 through D-04 and D-07; create redirect/archive/replacement plans as required; preserve evidence. | Route new member/client traffic to an unresolved surface or “fix” a business decision with a cosmetic change. | Each P0 surface has an approved role, a source-deployment match, and a written remediation/release plan. |
| **2. Public front-door integrity** | `beaconmomentum.com` | Audit all navigation, cards, footer destinations, schema, offers, policies, support routes, disclosures, mobile states, and cross-property handoffs against the parent ledger. Hold/label unresolved destination cards. | Amplify any P0 destination or imply a shared account/data relationship. | Every surviving public CTA has a ledger record, working destination, ownership label, disclosure, and responsive validation. |
| **3. Property-specific rehabilitation** | Community, Trading, Labs, related app, affiliates | Implement approved property role, offer, policy, routing, and component corrections in the confirmed source repository. Follow independent repository → test → commit → CI/CD workflow. | Shared headers, uniform product cards, or referral paths that obscure a property’s own legal, account, or service boundary. | Per-property desktop/mobile, route, claim, policy, account, and deployment evidence is complete. |
| **4. Shared design and handoff system** | Relaunch-ready properties only | Apply shared parent strip, role-aware header/footer, disclosure block, offer snapshot, destination card, support/policy rail, semantic tokens, accessible focus/motion, and pathway accents. | Clone one property’s full layout or dominant palette onto another; use stock claims or generic social proof. | Brand, contrast, keyboard, mobile, disclosure, and cross-property handoff acceptance criteria pass. |
| **5. Relaunch validation and staged traffic** | All cleared properties | Validate live desktop/mobile routes, links, support/policy rails, pricing, source code, analytics, forms in non-submitting mode, and external handoff disclosure. Increase traffic only per cleared destination. | A big-bang relaunch or public “all sites are aligned” claim while a P0/P1 route remains unresolved. | Owner sign-off, evidence board update, staged go-live record, and rollback/incident contacts. |

## 6. Shared components and acceptance criteria

The common foundation applies **only after** the property passes its role and route gates. It should produce recognizably related experiences without erasing role-specific expression.

| Component | Required behavior | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|
| **Parent strip** | Names the property relationship without implying shared login, commerce, data, or service ownership. | On every applicable property it is legible, keyboard reachable, mobile-safe, and factually accurate. |
| **Destination card** | States destination audience, role, external/referral status, and what will not transfer automatically. | The link matches the parent ledger, opens the approved route, and has no hidden prefill or record merge. |
| **Offer snapshot** | States source-verified name, price/starting-price or custom-pricing label, scope boundary, and CTA. | Price, scope, link, terms/support route, and status match the active offer ledger exactly. |
| **Disclosure block** | Appears near financial-education/simulation, health-adjacent, service, payment, privacy, and data actions. | Contrast, mobile layout, proximity, wording, and destination links pass review. |
| **Support and policy rail** | Exposes privacy, terms, cancellation/refund, contact/support, and relevant disclaimers. | Every link works publicly, names the responsible entity, and matches current operations. |
| **Role-aware navigation** | Shows only navigation relevant to the property’s approved role. | No member/client/app action appears on a public discovery site without an explicit approved handoff. |

## 7. Relaunch readiness test

No property may be labeled “ready” until each answer is **yes**:

1. Is the deployed source and operating owner confirmed?
2. Is the public role, audience, primary action, and account boundary approved?
3. Do every published offer, price, claim, testimonial, and CTA match an approved source of truth?
4. Do terms, privacy, support, refund/cancellation, and relevant disclosure routes work and name the correct responsible entity?
5. Does each external handoff explain the destination and preserve data/account separation?
6. Are desktop and mobile navigation, keyboard focus, contrast, reduced motion, route behavior, and non-submitting form states validated?
7. Are analytics, consent, payment, CRM, member/client data, human review, and incident/rollback paths appropriate to the property?

## 8. Program recommendation

The immediate next executable tranche is **Wave 0: freeze and source control**, followed by the owner decisions in Section 3. The public-front-door audit may proceed in parallel as read-only/ledger-backed work, but no broad relaunch, new traffic source, design-system propagation, or cross-property conversion path should be released until the P0 role, price, route, policy, and data conflicts are resolved.

This sequence deliberately prioritizes trust and operational correctness over surface polish. Once the decision inventory is cleared, implementation can proceed independently in the confirmed repositories under the standard clone → test → commit → CI/CD verification workflow, with desktop and mobile cross-property acceptance checks before each property is promoted.[2] [5]
